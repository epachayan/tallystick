E2  LEAKAGE UNDER ADAPTIVE PROBING
==============================================================================
vocabulary 64, committed scope 8, 2000 trials

scheme                    mean bits learned   max     as % of set
B5 dispute-driven         64.0                64      100.0%
B6 accountable queries    7.2                 35      11.3%

predicted mean probes before refutation  (V+1)/(k+1) = 7.22
measured                                              = 7.21

The bound is NOT the query budget. It is the adversary's supply of
truthful grievances: probing stops at the first in-scope hit, which
arrives after (V+1)/(k+1) probes in expectation. Denser scopes are
cheaper to protect; sparse ones leak more before refutation.

E3  CROSSOVER: CLOSED FORM vs MEASURED
==============================================================================
item length L = 13.9 B, roots 2R = 64 B, queries q = 1
(2 signatures appear in both schemes and cancel)

    n   full 2nL+M   selective      winner
    2           96         124        full
    4          151         155        full
    8          263         187   selective
   12          374         223   selective
   16          485         365   selective
   24          708         254   selective
   32          931         429   selective
   64         1821         496   selective

predicted crossover  n* = 8
Empirical sweep placed it between 4 and 8 (1.06x at 4, 0.90x at 8).

Closed form, q queries:
    n* = smallest n with   2nL + M  >  2R + q*P(n),
    P(n) = 2 + L + ceil(log2 n)*32 + ceil(log2 n)/8   per non-membership proof

Reading: the threshold is set by the FIXED commitment overhead (2R),
not by the log factor. Selective adjudication pays once the record
exceeds a few items, and degrades linearly in q -- so the design rule
is to bound q, which is exactly what B5 does and B4 does not.

==============================================================================
V=64, k=8, 20000 trials

quantile    measured probes   closed-form P(>t)

Closed form for a high-percentile bound: the smallest t with
    C(V-k, t) / C(V, t)  <=  1 - p
    p95: leakage <= 19 probes  (30% of vocabulary)
    p99: leakage <= 27 probes  (42% of vocabulary)

BUDGETED DEFENCE (B8): cap probes per epoch at b.
budget b    mean leaked   p99 leaked    % of vocab
1           1.00          1             1.6%
2           1.87          2             3.1%
4           3.28          4             6.2%
8           5.14          8             12.5%
16          6.72          16            25.0%

A per-epoch budget converts an expectation guarantee into a hard cap.
Accountability costs nothing: an honest complainant needs one query per
real grievance, so b can be small without impeding legitimate disputes.

Detection rate minus false-accusation rate is zero at every p, to

marks a sincerely mistaken party as culpable in 100 of 475 scenarios.

THE BOUNDARY (from the M1/M16 row, which does NOT match):
  M6/M15 and M7/M17 are identical because the malicious version needs
  only an ASSERTION -- claim a different scope, deny a receipt. Nothing
  distinguishes a liar from someone who misremembers.
  M1/M16 differs because the malicious version needs an act of
  CONCEALMENT -- the liar omits the action from its own record, the
  good-faith actor reports it. Concealment leaves a trace; belief does not.

  So: mistake is separable from malice exactly when malice requires an
  additional concealing act. Where malice is purely assertional, no
  evidence-based scheme can attribute fault.

n       max undetected rate     misconduct per 100 disputes
10      0.54                    54
20      0.42                    42
50      0.37                    37
100     0.32                    32

Statefulness does NOT escape T5. It converts a MISS into a FALSE
ACCUSATION -- exactly T1's dichotomy, reproduced at the level of rates
rather than single disputes. The honest-but-unlucky executor and the
disciplined offender have overlapping observed distributions, so no
threshold separates them.

What statefulness DOES buy is a bound: an adversary that stays below
the detection threshold has capped its own misconduct at roughly the
honest base failure rate. It cannot hide unlimited concealment -- only
concealment indistinguishable from bad luck.

T1 is therefore SCALE-INVARIANT: moving from single-dispute evidence
to pattern evidence does not escape the accuracy/completeness
dichotomy, it reinstates it one level up.

Deterrence requires S >= D. At S = D = 100 the honest executor pays

THE CONSERVATION
response                  currency of the cost          who pays
single-state (B1)         false accusations             honest, wrongly blamed
three-state (B3-B10)      missed detections             victims of concealment
stateful (B11)            false accusations at rate     honest but unlucky
bonded (B12)              forfeited stake               honest, taxed p0*D

Bonding does not escape the dichotomy either. It converts a
CLASSIFICATION ERROR into a TAX. Every response pays for the same
indistinguishability in a different currency -- and the honest party
pays in three of the four.

T8 (CONSERVATION): where evidence cannot separate honest failure from
concealment, the cost of that ambiguity cannot be eliminated, only
denominated -- in wrongful blame, in undetected misconduct, or in a
levy on the innocent.

At a 1-in-100,000 dispute rate, adjudication is under 0.001% of total

The deployed default is ~12x cheaper in steady state, which is the
actual reason it persists -- not ignorance of the accountability
literature. A scheme is adopted on its idle cost, not its dispute cost.

QUALITATIVE CLAIM: does statefulness always trade a miss for a
false accusation, or only near p0 = 0.25?

  false-accusation rate > 0 at every p0 tested : True
  detection power < 1 at every p0 tested       : True
  -> T7's qualitative claim is ROBUST

What DOES move with p0 is the adversary's misconduct ceiling: it
tracks p0 almost proportionally, so the tolerable rate of undetected
concealment is set entirely by how often honest parties genuinely
fail. A system with reliable storage bounds its adversary tightly;
a flaky one hands the adversary the same slack it gives the unlucky.

That is a NEW consequence of T7, not visible at a single p0:
  the price of unreliable infrastructure is paid twice -- once in
  honest failures, and again in the concealment those failures cover.
